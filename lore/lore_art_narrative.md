# MMO Art & Narrative Bible

## 1. The Setting: "The Exile Frontier"
Apex Outlaw is set in **Helion** — a fresh, barely-touched star system on the far side of one colossal jump gate from an overpopulated Sol. The Federation (FED) ships its surplus people and its convicts through that gate and leaves them to fend for themselves; everyone you meet is some flavor of cast-out, and almost nothing is built yet. The look should read **young, improvised, and underbuilt** — first foundries, salvage depots, and exile shanty-hubs, not gleaming civilization. See [`lore_story_bible.md`](lore_story_bible.md) §1 for the premise.

Space is structured around specialized planetary bodies, avoiding empty lightyears in favor of dense, localized "Sectors." Deep space is largely untraversable; ships use the **Jump Gate Network** — one ancient precursor gate beside every major body, with proximity-based bubble bonds that **rewrite the connection graph in real time as the bodies orbit** — while the great **Custos Gateway** is the only (FED-controlled) door back to Sol. See [`lore_story_bible.md`](lore_story_bible.md) §5.

### The Planetary Rules:
- Bases are not built on deep gravity-well planets — escaping orbit is too expensive to be worth it.
- Hubs and shanty-towns cluster in low-gravity environments: **moons, asteroids, and micro-planets.**

## 2. The Great Factions (Visuals & Lore)

### A. FED — Federation (The Warden Beyond the Gate)
- **Lore/Vibe:** The bureaucratic authority that *sentenced you here.* FED runs the Custos Gateway, processes inbound exile fleets, registers every Seed, and skims the resource flow back to Sol. Powerful but **thin on the ground** — its in-system face is Concordia's customs/registry and the occasional FED Police sweep. Heavy taxes, high security, distant cruelty. (Canonical name: **FED** — see [`lore_story_bible.md`](lore_story_bible.md).)
- **Visual Aesthetic:**
  - Sleek, bureaucratic, mass-produced. White hulls, blue energy emissions.
  - UI colors lean toward crisp holographics (Cyan, White).
  - Ships lack exposed wiring; everything is armored in clean panels — institutional, not improvised.

### B. ICE — Iron Core (The Inmates Who Took the Prison)
- **Lore/Vibe:** Renegade exiles who organized, seized the first foundries, and made themselves the de-facto government of the colony floor. They run the convict population FED keeps shipping in. Ruthless, meritocratic, industrial — power earned at the forge or the gun. Stronghold: Ferrum / Civitas Ferri.
- **Visual Aesthetic:**
  - Harsh angles, thick ablative armor. Gunmetal grey with oxidized iron-red striping.
  - UI colors are purely functional (Amber, Orange, Muted Reds).
  - Ships resemble flying tanks or submarines — heavy plate over salvaged industrial frames.

### C. The Outlaws (The Twice-Cast-Out)
- **Lore/Vibe:** Exiles who kneel to neither FED nor ICE. Cast out of Sol, then out of the Iron Core's order, they live off the grid in the deep belts on the Black Market and hidden Citadels.
- **Visual Aesthetic:**
  - Kit-bashed, exposed wires, asymmetrical designs.
  - Colors are haphazard — yellow hazard tape, mismatched unpainted scrap.
  - Engines produce dirty, volatile thrust plumes rather than clean plasma.

## 3. Technology Limits
A crucial aesthetic pillar of the game: **Technology is dangerous, not magical.**

- Lasers do not make "pew pew" noises; they emit low, terrifying hums of sheer heat.
- Kinetic weapons physically jolt the ship backward upon firing.
- Radar is not omniscient; it gets fuzzy and unreliable near Silicate Dust clouds or Plasma Storms.
- "Jammers" shouldn't look like a spell effect—they look like violent, tearing static on the enemy player's screen.
