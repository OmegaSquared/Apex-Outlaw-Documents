# 🗺️ APEX OUTLAW — ROADMAP

*Updated July 2026. The order is the plan — the dates aren't promises.*

**Apex Outlaw is a sandbox space MMO where the economy is the game.** No XP, no level grind. Progression is gear, knowledge, territory, and reputation — all built on one rule: every pilot perceives the universe's resources differently, so the only way to profit from someone else's discoveries is to trade with them.

---

## ✅ Recently Landed

- **Design-your-own ships** — the holographic Blueprint Design Studio. Snap structural parts together with a magnetic feel; the power core's energy budget is the only referee ("the game balances itself"). Design → build → fly works end to end.
- **Shipyard 2.0** — live rotatable 3D ship view, hardpoint slots, weapon fitting.
- **Mining sandbox** — cutter drones, gas harvesting, scanning & assay, full drone fleet command.
- **Weapon test range** — fire real weapons against real AI to tune your fits before combat.
- Real persistent accounts, crash capture, and the in-game **F8 bug reporter** that posts straight to this Discord.

## 🎯 NEXT UP — Closed Alpha

The first invited testers get persistent accounts in the live world:

- Log in and see the **living sector map** — jump gates drift in and out of reach as planets really orbit.
- **The full economic loop:** mine graded ore → haul it → sell at a faction hub → buy modules → fit your ship.
- **First combat:** PvP duels and NPC pirate ambushes in server-authoritative instances (3v3 + spectators — space battles have an audience).
- Take damage, pay for repairs, chat, log out — and resume exactly where you left off.

The alpha bar: one mining loop + one combat loop, rock solid.

## 📈 PHASE 2 — The Living Economy

- **Real market exchange** — full order books (bid/ask, limit, stop) on every hub.
- **Physical trading** — goods sit where they're listed. Cargo never teleports. Haulers become a profession.
- **Freight contracts** — escrow-backed shipping with collateral and hauler reputation.
- **The Maker's Mark** — every crafted item carries its forger's name forever.
- **NPC convoys & miners** keep markets liquid — and give pirates predictable prey.

---

## ⚔️ PHASE 3 — Alliances & Territory

- **Uncapped alliances** — 3-pilot crews to 1,000+ coalitions, with ranks, departments, and squadrons.
- **Territory as physics** — bubble radii around your citadels, not painted maps. Control emerges from what you hold.
- **Formal wardecs** — and the most dramatic rule in the game: **lose a war and your alliance's name is retired from the server forever.** The winner earns the permanent Warlord title.
- **Faction sovereignty** — a player alliance can literally replace the Federation on a planet. Same rules for everyone.

---

## 🌍 PHASE 4 — The Three-Layer World

- **Low Orbit** — full-3D planets with capital ships, orbital citadels, drydocks, and RTS fleet control.
- **Surface** — ground bases as real structures on real terrain: mine → smelter → forge → shipyard.
- **Activity-noise stealth** — a running factory broadcasts radar noise; a silent base stays dark. Throughput vs. exposure, your call.
- Capital ships, towing, ramming, and close-combat naval warfare.
- Black markets, contraband, laundering, and the hacking module suite.
